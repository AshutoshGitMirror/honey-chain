Honey Chain — how data moves, with one hash link

Less jargon: one harvest, one hash, one QR at the farm. After that we add notes that link to the first hash. KVIC network adds to it, no one rewrites it.

flowchart TD
  A[Household farm - 10 boxes
  village, flower, date
  makes one hash] --> B[QR at field
  hash is fingerprint
  prev_hash links to last harvest]

  B --> C[Cooperative pickup
  notes who, when, weight
  links to QR]

  C --> D[Processing plant
  van at doorstep, 300kg in 8h
  heats 60-65C and filters]

  D --> E[Lab at NABL
  15 tests, adds cert PDF
  and CA number]

  E --> F[Authorized packer
  writes label after lab
  AGMARK + lot + MRP]

  F --> G[KVIC Bhavan shop
  puts bottle on shelf]

  G --> H[Consumer at shop
  scans QR, sees household, flower,
  google map of farm, cert badge]

  H -. verify hash chain .-> B

Flexible: if we pool many households for one plant run, the plant lot lists many field hashes. Label is written after the lab, not before. Household pin is everywhere, same coords for customer and KVIC.
